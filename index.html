Hero Me Builder Instructions

  • Click and drag to rotate the view
  • Scroll to zoom in/out
  • Click on the purple attachment points to add parts
  • Double-click on parts to remove them
  • Use the blue up/down arrows to adjust part cooling ducts and probe mounts
  • If things don't line up correctly (which is common), click the move icon (four arrows) then click the problematic model to translate it
  • Click the "Download Assembly" button to save your work
